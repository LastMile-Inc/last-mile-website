# Source and Citation Policy

## Source hierarchy

1. Standards organizations, regulators, government laboratories, and official specifications.
2. Original equipment manufacturer technical documentation for equipment-specific behavior.
3. Peer-reviewed research and recognized industrial institutes.
4. Vendor technical papers, clearly identified as vendor material.
5. Secondary analysis only when a primary source is unavailable.

## Requirements

- Link directly to the supporting page or document.
- Record title, organization, URL, access date, and supported proposition.
- Do not present a facility-specific example as a universal standard.
- Do not copy protected standards text. Summarize public descriptions and cite the official landing page.
- Vendor material may establish technical plausibility, not vendor-independent universality.
- Recheck sources before major publication and at least annually.

## Industrial reference disclosure

Use this site note where scenario thresholds appear:

> Reference values describe a controlled Last Mile demonstration. External guidance is identified where applicable; equipment limits, commissioned setpoints, operating bands, response authority, and acceptance criteria remain customer- and site-specific.
