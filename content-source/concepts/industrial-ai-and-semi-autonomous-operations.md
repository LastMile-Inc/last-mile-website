# Industrial AI and Semi-Autonomous Operations

## Canonical position

Industrial AI requires governed context, explicit authority, current valid evidence, and verified outcomes. Last Mile supports a progression from deterministic automation to assisted and bounded agent action without treating model output as operational truth.

## Publication-ready article

### Industrial AI must know what it is allowed to do, and whether it worked

An industrial model can identify a pattern, summarize evidence, recommend a response, or plan a sequence. None of those outputs should become an operating fact simply because a model produced it. Physical operations add constraints that ordinary enterprise automation can avoid: stale sensors, uncertain identities, safety boundaries, degraded redundancy, physical work, and outcomes that must be measured after the action.

Last Mile's architecture gives industrial AI a governed substrate. Singularity provides canonical assets, topology, Conditions, evidence, quality, history, and prior Outcomes. Infinit-Flow supplies policy, approvals, timers, permitted actions, receipts, and stop conditions. Infinit-Control makes the evidence, recommendation, authority, and result legible to the operator.

This produces three clear modes. AUTO executes a bounded digital step already authorized by policy. ASSIST assembles evidence or proposes a response while an operator decides. HUMAN AUTHORITY retains control over physical work, safety, LOTO, and customer-governed changes. A model or agent must declare which mode applies.

The most important learning signal comes after the action. Did pressure return to band? Did drawdown recover across three cycles? Did the same impairment recur? Outcome evidence makes improvement possible without rewarding an agent for merely completing a task.

CESMII describes smart manufacturing as proactive and semi-autonomous, with automated decisions for routine situations and people involved in non-routine ones. Last Mile's position is aligned but precise: autonomy should increase only as identity, evidence, policy, exception handling, and outcome verification become stronger.

## What this looks like in an operating response

At `LS-07`, current-rate arithmetic shows approximately 06:40 to the 9.0 ft high-high level if the observed +0.18 ft/min rise continues. That is a disclosed linear estimate, not a predictive model. An ASSIST function can assemble the wet-well, pump, flow, standby, and owner context; customer policy retains physical and control authority. The result is learned only after three complete drawdown cycles satisfy the configured recovery contract.
