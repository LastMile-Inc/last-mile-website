# Infinit-Signal Page Contract and Copy

## Job

Show how Infinit-Signal connects to the production ecosystem already in place and provides a fast, scalable path for trusted operational data to enter Last Mile. Do not turn the page into a connector catalog or claim measured production performance.

## Audience

OT, controls, data-platform, reliability, and operations leaders who need to connect a complex brownfield environment without replacing the systems that already run it.

## Five-second promise

One fast path from your plant floor into Last Mile.

## Narrative limits

- Major sections: 4
- Narrative copy: 450-650 words
- Primary visual: a high-throughput signal intake engine connecting existing source families to Singularity
- Supporting interaction: restrained animated data movement with a reduced-motion-safe state

## Page order

1. Speed: meet the production ecosystem where it is.
2. Compatibility: understand the source and map it without erasing its meaning.
3. UNS: consume the customer's live communication fabric without taking it over.
4. Scale: move current critical traffic ahead of lower-priority telemetry and backfill, then recover deferred data safely.

## Section contracts

### Meet the production ecosystem already in place

Explain the shortest safe implementation path: understand the source, preserve its meaning, and start with the smallest useful scope. Show MQTT/UNS, Industrial IoT and controls, historians and APIs, and governed files and mapping packages as source families. Named products remain representative, not certified integrations.

### Read the UNS without taking it over

**Required claim:** The UNS remains the customer's communication and discovery fabric.

Begin by acknowledging the time, engineering judgment, and plant knowledge required to create and maintain a useful UNS. Explain that Infinit-Signal consumes configured MQTT/UNS subscriptions and preserves broker, publisher, topic, session, timestamp, quality, and replay context. Explain separately that JSON, repository-managed files, and spreadsheets may supply governed schemas, mappings, and configuration. Do not imply that a Git repository or spreadsheet is itself a UNS.

### Built to keep up when the plant gets loud

Explain priority-aware 24x7 ingestion through a visual in which critical events advance first, operating state follows, standard telemetry is regulated, and high-rate or backfill data waits safely. Connect the priority order to backpressure isolation, burst handling, store-and-forward recovery, and visible acceptance classifications. State that performance and stress testing use declared sustained rate, burst rate, payload size, source concurrency, recovery, and store-and-forward profiles. Do not publish universal rate, latency, availability, or production-grade claims without approved evidence.

## Responsive behavior

The hero intake visual becomes a vertically ordered source-to-Singularity path. Performance lanes remain readable without horizontal page overflow; a contained scroll region is acceptable for the detailed pipeline at 390px.

## Exclusions

- No detailed use-case evidence envelope in the hero.
- No validated connector, certified compatibility, universal scale, uptime, or latency claim.
- No implication that Last Mile replaces or operates the customer's UNS, brokers, control systems, or historians.
- No long quality-state card grid or repeated platform narrative.
