# Industrial Concepts Collection - Page Contract and Copy

## Job

Create one authoritative editorial home for industrial concepts that matter to Last Mile. Product pages should link to these explanations instead of independently redefining the terms.

## Hero

**Eyebrow:** INDUSTRIAL CONCEPTS

**H1:** The architectures shaping connected, intelligent, accountable operations.

**Body:** Clear explanations of the standards, operating models, and industrial ideas that influence Last Mile—where they help, where their boundaries remain, and how they connect to verified physical outcomes.

## Collection order and card copy

1. **UNS and SSOM: moving industrial data is not the same as making it accountable**
   The customer UNS distributes current information. SSOM preserves identity, meaning, evidence, and lifecycle continuity.

2. **Connected is not yet interoperable**
   Why protocols move values but semantic interoperability preserves operational meaning.

3. **OPC UA and Companion Specifications**
   Preserve strong source models and connect them to the cross-system operating outcome.

4. **Digital twins, AAS, and operational memory**
   Asset representation and accountable response history solve different, complementary problems.

5. **The digital thread should not stop at the work order**
   Last Mile's operational outcome thread connects evidence, response, return measurements, and recurrence.

6. **Industrial AI must know what it is allowed to do—and whether it worked**
   Governed context, authority, exception handling, and verified outcomes are prerequisites for responsible agents.

7. **Industry 5.0 and human authority**
   Human-centricity, sustainability, and resilience require more than faster automation.

8. **OT security and explicit trust boundaries**
   Connectivity must preserve physical performance, reliability, safety, least privilege, and safe degradation.

9. **Industrial data spaces and sovereign learning**
   Cross-company insight without surrendering participant control or making consent implicit.

10. **Composable manufacturing beyond the connector**
    Discoverable capabilities still require semantics, commissioning, policy, and outcome verification.

11. **The dark factory is not a responsibility-free factory**
    Less routine human presence increases the need for trustworthy evidence and accountable recovery.

## Editorial rules

- Use standards and recognized industrial sources.
- State Last Mile's interpretation separately from external definitions.
- Include a clear boundary and prohibited implication.
- Do not turn a concept article into an unqualified product roadmap claim.
- Update the canonical concept file first; cards and cross-links consume it.

- Response, authority, and industrial AI.

Every article includes one compact plant-level operating-response example sourced from a canonical use case or governed reference architecture. Public pages render claim posture in natural language rather than exposing internal maturity enums.
