# Resources Hub Contract and Copy

## Job

Establish Last Mile as a technically credible guide to physical-operations architecture and help enterprise architects, plant managers, and OT engineers reach the most useful current material quickly.

## Audience

Enterprise architects, plant managers, reliability leaders, controls engineers, and OT engineers who already understand industrial systems and want practical architecture, operating boundaries, and evidence instead of trend commentary.

## Five-second promise

Last Mile publishes grounded thinking about the difficult space between industrial signals, operational meaning, accountable work, and measured results.

## Narrative limits

- Major sections: 5
- Narrative copy: 350 to 550 words plus resource metadata
- Primary visual: physical industrial equipment intersecting with a precise source-to-operating-meaning schematic
- Supporting interactions: direct podcast, article, proof, archive, RSS, and contact links only

## Page order

1. Hero: The Intelligence Hub for Physical Operations.
2. Featured property: Signal 2 Action.
3. Industrial architecture concepts: UNS, SSOM, semantic interoperability, and open protocols.
4. Engineering and proof: configured protocol intake, governed security controls, declared workload profiles, and cooling redundancy.
5. Historical Context & Archives with the route-specific subscription action.

## Section contracts

### Signal 2 Action

- Make the podcast the visual anchor directly below the hero.
- Use the official cover art from public/signal_2_action.jpg.
- Present a premium media-console composition whose controls are real links, not nonfunctional player decoration.
- Explain that the series covers signal trust, authority, workflow design, governed automation, and measured operations with operators, engineers, integrators, builders, and enterprise leaders.
- Link to the podcast route and RSS feed.

### Industrial Architecture Concepts

- Present three substantial editorial entries: UNS and SSOM, semantic interoperability, and open protocol integration.
- Spell out SSOM as Standardized Semantic Object Model on first use.
- State that the customer UNS remains the real-time communication and discovery fabric. Infinit-Signal preserves its source context and prepares SSOM-conformant records for Singularity.
- Explain that protocol connectivity is necessary but does not establish canonical identity, time, quality, lineage, authority, or measured recovery.
- Name OPC UA, Modbus, BACnet, MQTT, and Sparkplug as configured source families without implying certified or generally available connectors.
- Use the generated semantic-architecture visual as orientation. Keep every required definition and link in accessible HTML.

### Engineering and Proof

- Replace vague product updates with three inspectable paths.
- Declared workload profile: sustained rate, burst rate, payload size, source concurrency, recovery, and store-and-forward behavior through Infinit-Signal.
- Governed security controls: tenant isolation, workload identity, encryption, audit, policy, least privilege, and segmentation-aware deployment. Do not recast these controls as a productized storage claim.
- Cooling redundancy reference: link to the canonical data-center cooling use case and keep work closure distinct from measured recovery.
- Declared workload targets remain engineering targets until matching repeatable benchmark evidence is approved. Do not present target throughput or zero-loss language as achieved production metrics.
- Use the generated protocol-proof visual as orientation and keep the architecture labels in accessible HTML.

### Historical Context & Archives

- Keep legacy company releases, Knowledge Conference material, and the prior vendor-focused podcast chapter accessible at the bottom.
- Historical material must be visibly separated from current architecture and cannot be used as proof of current platform capability.
- Link to the company newsroom and Signal 2 Action archive.
- Include the exact route-specific heading: Subscribe to our architectural briefs and podcast.
- Provide working links for the RSS feed and architecture-contact path.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop uses an asymmetrical editorial grid with the podcast as the largest composition.
- Tablet stacks the hero and architecture visuals above their text while preserving clear article hierarchy.
- Mobile uses one column, keeps every link at least 44 pixels high, and removes decorative connector motion.
- No horizontal scrolling is required at any supported viewport.
