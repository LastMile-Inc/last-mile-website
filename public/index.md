# Last Mile — Run your operation as one.

Software for industrial operations. Last Mile brings information from your equipment and business systems into one place. It uses AI to help your team spot problems and decide what to do next. Your team can assign work, automate approved tasks, and check that the equipment is working as expected afterward.

## Seeing a problem is only the start

A warning doesn’t fix the problem. Your team still has to piece together what happened, find the right person, and make sure the fix worked. Last Mile helps them manage that work without chasing updates across different systems.

- Understand the problem. See the warning alongside the equipment’s recent readings and history.
- Get the right people moving. Assign the next step and get approval when it’s needed.
- Check that the fix worked. Check the equipment’s readings after the work to see whether it is running as expected.

Example: a cooling unit gets too warm. The team checks its recent readings, assigns someone to investigate, and checks the temperature again after the work.

## Built for the operations you’re responsible for

Keep production moving, keep cooling equipment working, and get the right people involved when something needs attention. Example uses for manufacturing, data centers, and cold storage.
