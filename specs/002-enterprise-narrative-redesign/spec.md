# Feature 002: Enterprise narrative and structural redesign

## Purpose

Turn the refreshed corporate website into a more credible enterprise sales narrative for enterprise architects, plant managers, and OT engineers while preserving controlled product behavior, claim boundaries, customer authority, and safety controls.

## User need

Readers need to understand why disconnected industrial systems lose accountability, how the four Last Mile products address that failure, and what each role can see or do without encountering repeated sections, broken layouts, or decorative graphics that obscure the story.

## Scope

- Tighten global section spacing and keep the approved light, pale-blue, steel, grey, and white visual system.
- Reframe the Home bottling-line story as an architectural failure and make the daily-problem section text-only.
- Rebuild the Accountable Operations Loop as a responsive three-column composition with a horizontal Condition, Response, Outcome sequence.
- Replace the Platform accountability graphic and remove the repeated learning-platform section.
- Correct Infinit-Signal scale wrapping while strengthening source-agnostic edge-to-enterprise intake language.
- Reframe Singularity, Infinit-Flow, and Infinit-Control around their requested enterprise roles and boundaries.
- Keep Use Cases hero media beside its copy on desktop.
- Update governance tests, page contracts, feature records, and browser expectations.

## Out of scope

- Calculations, data processing, saved formats, security behavior, customer separation, permissions, ownership, extension rules, safety controls, deployment, merge, cloud resources, or GCP changes.
- Claims of certified connectors, autonomous physical control, deployed customer interfaces, universal performance, or guaranteed results.
- Dark or black site sections that conflict with the approved refreshed-site palette.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All three commissioned visuals use the approved light palette and load at their native resolution.
2. Home, Platform, Infinit-Signal, Singularity, Infinit-Flow, Infinit-Control, and Use Cases implement the requested narrative and structural changes.
3. The Accountable Operations Loop presents three stages on each side of a centered light-theme graphic, with the center progression remaining on one line.
4. The Platform route contains no repeated Learning Operations Platform section.
5. The Infinit-Control command portal is clearly labeled as a concept, uses role-specific views from C-suite to shift worker, and retains one shared operating context.
6. Desktop and phone layouts have no hidden controls, overlap, or horizontal overflow; keyboard controls keep visible focus and useful accessible names.
7. Governance, editorial, content, type, lint, test, build, published-output, file-format, and whitespace checks pass.
